# Indian Compounder Index (ICI) Scoring Framework

## Vision

The Indian Compounder Index (ICI) aims to identify high-quality Indian companies that have the potential to create long-term shareholder wealth.

Unlike traditional stock screeners, ICI focuses on business quality rather than short-term price movements. Every company is evaluated using transparent and explainable metrics so that the final score can always be understood.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of ICI are:

- Build a structured database of all listed Indian companies.
- Collect reliable financial and business information.
- Calculate standardized investment metrics.
- Assign an explainable ICI Score to every company.
- Rank companies based on long-term investment quality.
- Generate research reports for investors.

---

# Investment Philosophy

ICI is designed for long-term investing.

The framework favors businesses with:

- Consistent revenue growth
- Consistent profit growth
- High return on capital
- Strong balance sheets
- Honest and capable management
- Sustainable competitive advantages
- Predictable cash flows
- Reasonable valuations

ICI does not attempt to predict short-term stock price movements.

---

# Scoring Categories

The overall ICI Score is composed of multiple categories.

## 1. Business Quality

Purpose:

Evaluate the strength and durability of the underlying business.

Examples:

- Market leadership
- Competitive advantage
- Industry position
- Brand strength

---

## 2. Growth

Purpose:

Measure the company's ability to grow over time.

Examples:

- Revenue Growth
- Profit Growth
- EPS Growth

---

## 3. Financial Strength

Purpose:

Measure financial stability.

Examples:

- ROE
- ROCE
- Debt to Equity
- Interest Coverage
- Free Cash Flow

---

## 4. Management Quality

Purpose:

Evaluate management alignment with shareholders.

Examples:

- Promoter Holding
- Promoter Pledge
- Corporate Governance
- Share Dilution

---

## 5. Valuation

Purpose:

Avoid paying excessive prices for good companies.

Examples:

- PE Ratio
- PB Ratio
- EV/EBITDA
- PEG Ratio

---

# Explainability

Every score generated by ICI must be explainable.

Example:

ICI Score: 91/100

Business Quality    +20
Growth              +18
Financial Strength  +19
Management          +17
Valuation           +17

Total               91

The system should never generate scores without explaining the calculation.

- Data before AI.
